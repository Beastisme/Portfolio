About

I'm a Civil Engineering graduate from Dedan Kimathi University of Technology, grounded in structural design, construction supervision and engineering documentation, gained through two industrial attachments with Kenyan government engineering departments.

Since August 2026 I have been a Site Engineer at EZAG Limited, where I am responsible for site safety and for making sure workmanship and specifications are followed.

Before that, from January to April 2025, I was attached to the structural engineering office at the State Department of Public Works. There I worked on the design of a three-storey classroom block for Pangani Girls High School: slab, beam, column and staircase design, plus the drawings and bar bending schedules (BBS). We also made site visits to check progress.

My first attachment, in 2023 at the Ministry of Water on the Partakilat Pan Development Project in Kilgoris, exposed me to survey work, laboratory tests, earthworks and design, including the spillway design.

My final year research assessed stormwater drainage performance in Nyeri Town, combining field survey and hydraulic analysis to flag flood-prone areas and recommend improvements.

Featured research

Dedan Kimathi University of Technology, Civil Engineering, 2026 Nyeri Town, Kenya

Assessment of the Performance of Stormwater Drainage Systems in Nyeri Town

Final year research project
  • Conducted hydraulic assessment of urban stormwater drainage infrastructure to evaluate system performance and capacity.
  • Identified drainage bottlenecks and flood-prone areas through field surveys and hydraulic analysis.
  • Developed engineering recommendations to improve urban stormwater management and reduce flood risk.
Hydraulic analysis Field survey Stormwater drainage Flood risk assessment

Why it matters

  • Urban drainage capacity directly determines a town's flood resilience during heavy rainfall.
  • Mapping bottlenecks in the field grounds recommendations in real, observed site conditions.
  • The findings offer a practical reference for future stormwater upgrades in Nyeri Town.
