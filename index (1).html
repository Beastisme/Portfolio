Who I Am

About Me

I'm a recent Civil Engineering graduate from Dedan Kimathi University of Technology, grounded in construction supervision, infrastructure development and engineering documentation gained through two attachments with Kenyan government engineering departments.

At the State Department of Public Works, I supported the supervision of public building projects — reviewing drawings and Bills of Quantities, inspecting works for quality and schedule compliance, and coordinating with multidisciplinary teams to resolve site issues.

At the Ministry of Water, on the Partakilat Pan Development Project in Kilgoris, I supported water infrastructure construction supervision — running QA/QC checks, verifying compliance with technical specifications, and preparing site reports.

My final year research assessed stormwater drainage performance in Nyeri Town, combining field survey and hydraulic analysis to flag flood-prone areas and recommend improvements. I'm now seeking a Graduate Engineer role where I can keep applying that mix of site-level precision and technical documentation.
